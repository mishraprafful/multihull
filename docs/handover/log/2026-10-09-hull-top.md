Rates are counter deltas between polls; the first frame shows dashes. A disconnect keeps the last frame, prints `router unreachable, retrying` and keeps polling; Ctrl-C exits 0.
- Router: `router_responses_total{route,status}` counted in `proxy/handler.rs` for every response, including error responses the router builds itself, because `router_requests_total` counts upstream attempts and had no client-visible status. Test `responses_are_counted_per_route_and_status` in `tests/proxy.rs`.
- State vocabulary follows the hero: `down` when the circuit is open, the probe reports down or health is down; `recovering` when half-open; `serving` otherwise. Failovers are attributed to a route through the provider of its endpoints (`router_failovers_total` carries `from` and `reason` only).

A `recovering` capture was attempted but half-open was never sampled within 90 s, so that state is covered by a unit test on `state_of` only.
